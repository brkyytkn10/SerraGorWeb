5 Ways to Make Your Immunity Rock-Solid This Winter

Category: SeasonalDate: December 1, 2025

When the weather turns cold, the first thing most of us think of is taking vitamin C. But the immune system isn't a simple mechanism that runs on vitamin C alone. Building a real shield against illness this winter takes a holistic strategy.

1. Check Your Vitamin D Levels

Since we get far less sun exposure in winter, our vitamin D stores deplete quickly. Vitamin D acts almost like a "commander" for our immune cells — when it's deficient, our defense line weakens. Getting a blood test and using supplements under medical supervision is one of the most important steps you can take in winter.

2. You Can't Skip Zinc

Zinc is an important mineral that blocks viruses from entering and multiplying inside cells. Pumpkin seeds, turkey, almonds and sesame seeds are all rich sources of zinc.

3. Phytotherapeutic Support: Echinacea and Elderberry

In phytotherapy, Echinacea can shorten the course of illness when used right as symptoms begin. Black Elderberry (Sambucus nigra) is known for its strong antiviral properties. However, people with autoimmune conditions should use echinacea with caution.

"70% of the immune system lives in the gut. That's why eating probiotic-rich foods like fermented pickles, kefir and yogurt can offer more protection than medication."

Dyt. Serra Gör's Winter Tea Recipe

My go-to recipe to soothe your throat and boost your resistance the moment you feel a cold coming on:

  • 1 pinch of linden flower
  • 1 slice of fresh ginger (about the size of your thumb tip)
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2-3 cloves
  • Half a lemon (with peel)

Steep in boiled water for 10 minutes; once it cools slightly, stir in 1 teaspoon of honey and drink.
