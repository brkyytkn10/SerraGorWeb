Functional Nutrition Consultation

Let's talk about the cause, not just the number on the scale.

The conventional approach often focuses on suppressing a symptom (weight gain, fatigue, bloating, acne). The functional approach asks: "Why did this symptom appear?" Is it an imbalance in your gut flora, ongoing inflammation, or a hormonal imbalance? In my consultations I follow these questions from a nutrition perspective and build your plan accordingly.

Who is it for?

  • People who say "I do everything right but nothing changes" in weight management
  • Those who constantly feel tired, low on energy or bloated
  • People with recurring digestive, skin or sleep complaints
  • Anyone who wants their blood tests interpreted together with their nutrition

How does it work?

  1. Detailed history and test review: I review your eating history, your lifestyle and, if you have them, your blood tests from the last 6 months.
  2. Root-cause work: I look at your complaints from the nutritional side of gut balance, inflammation and hormonal balance.
  3. Personalised nutrition plan: Based on the findings I prepare a plan that does not exclude the foods you love. Science-based herbal support can be added when appropriate.
  4. Follow-up: In weekly sessions we monitor how the plan works and update it.

How is it offered?

The functional approach is not a separate package; it is the perspective I apply in all of my consultation packages. Whichever package you choose, your plan is prepared with this approach of understanding the cause of your complaints. We can work online by video call or in person in Istanbul.

Why does it matter?

Suppressing a symptom can bring quick relief, but if the cause remains, the complaint can come back. My aim is not only to change the number on the scale, but to build a lasting nutrition routine that helps your body work in better balance and helps you feel better.

Things to keep in mind

As a dietitian I do not diagnose or make decisions about medication. Functional nutrition consultation does not replace your doctor's care; if you have a diagnosed condition or take medication, we prepare the plan together with your doctor, in consultation with them. The information on this page is for general information only.

Frequently asked questions

What is functional medicine?

It is an approach that looks at the cause of a complaint, not just its symptom. In nutrition consultation it means addressing gut balance, inflammation and hormonal balance through food.

Does it replace a doctor's check-up?

No. Diagnosis and treatment are the doctor's job; I support them on the nutrition side.

Is it a separate package?

No. The functional approach is applied in all of my consultation packages.

Do I need to bring blood tests?

I prefer to see your tests from the last 6 months. If you do not have any, I define the necessary parameters in our first call and guide you.

Can it be done online?

Yes, online by video call or in person in Istanbul.
