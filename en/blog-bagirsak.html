Our Second Brain, the Gut: What Is "Leaky Gut"?

Category: Functional MedicineDate: December 12, 2025

When Hippocrates said, centuries ago, "All disease begins in the gut," he was pointing toward the world of the microbiome that modern medicine is only now uncovering. Today, one of the most common patterns we see in functional medicine is "Leaky Gut Syndrome," or increased intestinal permeability.

What Is Leaky Gut?

Our intestinal wall is made up of cells tightly bound together by what are called "tight junctions." This structure normally allows only digested nutrients to pass into the bloodstream, while keeping toxins, bacteria and undigested large proteins out. But stress, poor nutrition, gluten sensitivity and unnecessary antibiotic use can damage this wall.

When the wall starts to "leak," substances that shouldn't enter the bloodstream do so anyway. The immune system attacks these foreign substances, and a general state of inflammation begins throughout the body.

Your Body May Be Sending You Signals

The symptoms of leaky gut aren't limited to stomach pain. If you're experiencing any of the following, the root cause may be your gut:

  • Constant fatigue and brain fog
  • Unexplained skin rashes, eczema, acne
  • Autoimmune conditions like Hashimoto's
  • Increasing food intolerances
  • Low or depressed mood
"Without healing the gut, it's nearly impossible to balance the immune system or your hormones."

The Repair Process: The 4R Protocol

In functional medicine, we apply the "4R Protocol" for gut repair:

  1. Remove: We eliminate inflammatory foods from the diet (usually gluten, dairy, sugar) through an elimination diet.
  2. Replace: We replace the enzymes and acids needed for proper digestion.
  3. Reinoculate: We support a healthy bacterial flora with probiotics and prebiotics.
  4. Repair: We use supports like glutamine, collagen (bone broth) and zinc to help repair the intestinal wall.
