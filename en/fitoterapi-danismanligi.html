Phytotherapy Consultation

The right herb, the right dose, the right person.

Phytotherapy is the use of plants through scientific methods and at the right dose. It is not "drink some herbal tea and see". In my consultations I plan herbal support together with your personalised nutrition plan. As a dietitian with a master's degree in phytotherapy, I look at the effect of both foods and plants on your body together.

Who is it for?

  • People who want to strengthen their immunity through nutrition and herbal support
  • Those looking for nutrition and herbal support for digestive concerns (bloating, gut regularity)
  • Anyone curious about natural support for stress, sleep and energy
  • People who take supplements or herbal teas but are unsure how much, when, and with what to combine them

How does it work?

  1. Getting to know you: I review your detailed medical-history form and, if you have them, your blood tests from the last 6 months. Your medications, supplements and health background become clear at this stage.
  2. Plan: Together we decide on science-based herbal support that fits your nutrition programme: which herb, in what form, and for how long.
  3. Follow-up: In weekly sessions I monitor the effects and how well you tolerate them, and update the plan when needed.

How is it offered?

Phytotherapy support is not a separate session; it is included within your nutrition consultation packages. In the same sessions we cover both your nutrition plan and your herbal support. We can work online by video call or in person in Istanbul.

What do we talk about?

  • Which herb is used for what purpose and in which form (tea, extract, capsule)
  • How much to take and how long to try it
  • Risk of interaction with your current medications
  • How it fits together with your nutrition plan

Things to keep in mind

Herbs, like medicines, need the right dose and are not suitable for everyone. In the following situations, herbal support is planned together with your doctor and with extra care:

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Thyroid conditions
  • Autoimmune diseases
  • Regular medication (especially blood thinners, sedatives, thyroid and immune medications)
  • An upcoming surgery

The information on this page is for general information only and does not replace diagnosis or treatment.

Frequently asked questions

What is phytotherapy?

It is the use of the healing parts, extracts and active compounds of plants through scientific methods. Choosing the right herb, at the right dose and in the right form is essential.

Does phytotherapy replace a nutrition programme?

No. It is an additional layer that supports your nutrition plan. Herbal support does not replace balanced nutrition, sleep and exercise.

I take medication. Can I use herbal support?

Some herbs can interact with medication. That is why at the start we review your medications and supplements together. Talking to your doctor before deciding is also recommended.

Can I get phytotherapy consultation online?

Yes. We work online by video call or in person in Istanbul.
