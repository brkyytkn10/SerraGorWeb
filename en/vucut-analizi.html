Body Composition Analysis

Let's see what the scale doesn't show.

A scale only shows your total weight. Two people of the same weight can have very different muscle, fat and water ratios. At our clinic in Maltepe, Istanbul, we measure your body composition in detail with the Tanita Perfecto and go through the results together in plain language.

Who is it for?

  • People wondering whether the number on the scale is muscle or fat
  • Those who do not want to lose muscle while losing weight
  • Those who want to build muscle mass
  • Anyone who wants a clear picture of their current state before starting a meal plan

What is measured?

  • Muscle, fat and water ratio
  • Visceral fat (fat around the internal organs)
  • Basal metabolic rate
  • Metabolic age

How is the measurement done?

  1. Appointment: In person at our clinic in Maltepe.
  2. Measurement: It takes about 5 minutes with the Tanita Perfecto.
  3. Interpretation: We go through the results together and talk about what they mean.

How to prepare

  • Come on an empty stomach (at least 3 hours after your last meal)
  • Do not drink water before the measurement
  • Do not exercise on the day of the measurement
  • You can come in your clothes; no other preparation is needed

Measurement and Analysis Package

The package includes 2 measurements with the Tanita Perfecto and 2 in-person assessment sessions. It does not include a regular meal plan or weekly WhatsApp follow-up; it is meant to clarify where you are now. In the packages that include a meal plan (In-Person, 2-Month, 3-Month) the measurement is part of the package; the Online Consultation package does not include Tanita measurement.

Things to keep in mind

The measurement works by passing a very weak electrical current through the body. For that reason, in the following situations the measurement is not done or the results are interpreted with caution:

  • The measurement is not done on people with a pacemaker or an implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD)
  • In pregnancy, during dialysis treatment, or with swelling (oedema), the reliability of the results may decrease; please let us talk about it first

Body analysis devices give an estimate, and results can vary slightly with your water balance during the day. That is why looking at the change over time is more meaningful than a single measurement. The measurement does not replace a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

Why is body analysis different from a scale?

A scale shows total weight; body analysis estimates how much of that weight is muscle, fat and water.

How long does the measurement take?

The measurement itself takes about 5 minutes.

I have a pacemaker. Can I be measured?

No. The current used by the device may affect the working of a pacemaker or an ICD, so the measurement is not done.

Can I get only the measurement, without a meal plan?

Yes. The Measurement and Analysis Package is exactly for this; it includes 2 measurements and 2 assessment sessions.

Is online measurement possible?

No. The measurement is done with the device at our clinic in Maltepe.
