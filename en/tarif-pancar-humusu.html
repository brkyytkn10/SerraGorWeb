Beetroot Hummus

Dip / Snack Prep: 15 min · Serves: 4-5

A step beyond classic hummus: beetroot adds a gentle sweetness and a vivid pink color. Serve it with sliced vegetables, whole-grain bread, or alongside a salad.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium cooked beetroots (about 300 g; vacuum-packed works too)
  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas (drained)
  • 2 tablespoons tahini
  • 1 small clove garlic
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 3-4 tablespoons water or chickpea liquid
  • Salt
  • To top: sesame seeds, chopped parsley

Method

  1. Cut the beetroot into large pieces; place it in a food processor or blender with the chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, cumin and salt.
  2. While blending, add water a little at a time until smooth and creamy.
  3. Taste and adjust the salt and lemon.
  4. Transfer to a serving bowl and top with olive oil, sesame seeds and parsley.
  5. It keeps in a covered container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
Tip: Served with carrot, cucumber, celery and bell pepper sticks, it makes a balanced snack in terms of both fiber and satiety. Beetroot stains your hands, so wear gloves if you want to avoid that!

Do not prepare this recipe if you are allergic to sesame or tahini. This recipe is for general information only. If you have allergies, a chronic condition or a specific nutrition plan, consult your dietitian before trying it.
