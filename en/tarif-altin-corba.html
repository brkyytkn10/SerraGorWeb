Golden Turmeric Red Lentil Soup

Soup Prep: 10 min · Cook: 25 min · Serves: 4
🥣

Everyone knows lentil soup, but with turmeric and ginger its color and aroma become something else entirely. It's made in one pot in half an hour, and tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils (rinsed)
  • 1 medium onion
  • 1 medium carrot
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon freshly grated ginger (optional)
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • 5 cups hot water
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Salt

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion and carrot, and crush the garlic.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pot over medium heat; saute the onion and carrot for 4-5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and stir for another minute.
  3. Add the turmeric, cumin, ginger and black pepper and stir for 30 seconds; once fragrant, add the lentils.
  4. Add the hot water and salt. Once it boils, lower the heat, partly cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Take off the heat and blend until smooth. If too thick, add a little hot water.
  6. Stir in the lemon juice at serving time and finish with a few drops of olive oil.
Tip: Black pepper helps increase the absorption of curcumin, the valuable compound in turmeric, and olive oil contributes as well, so I recommend not skipping either. For more on turmeric, see our turmeric article.

If you take blood-thinning medication or have a gallbladder condition, consult your doctor before consuming turmeric regularly. This recipe is for general information only. If you have allergies, a chronic condition or a specific nutrition plan, consult your dietitian before trying it.
