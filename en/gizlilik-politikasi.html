2. Personal Data We Process and How We Collect It

When you fill out a form on our Site or request an appointment, the following personal data is collected electronically through the relevant form:

  • Contact Form (Contact page): Full name, email address, message content.
  • Online Booking (Cal.com integration): Full name, email address, your preferred appointment date/time, and any appointment notes.
  • Site Usage Data: Cookie/tracking information collected through the Google Ads conversion tag (gtag.js), used to measure ad performance.

The guide in the "Download Your Free Guide" section is downloaded directly; no personal data is collected for this.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • To respond to your contact or appointment requests,
  • To manage and follow up on appointment scheduling,
  • To measure and improve site and advertising performance.

4. Legal Basis

Your personal data is processed, for contact and appointment requests, on the legal basis set out in KVKK Art. 5/2-(c), "being directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract." This Site does not send you any marketing or promotional emails.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

Your data is shared, only to the technical extent necessary, with the following service providers we use to deliver the Site's functionality. Some of these providers operate servers abroad (primarily in the United States), so this sharing may constitute an international transfer of personal data under KVKK:

  • Formspree (USA) — processing of the contact form,
  • Cal.com — online appointment scheduling and calendar integration,
  • Google LLC (USA) — advertising conversion tracking (Google Ads),
  • Turhost — the Turkey-based hosting provider for this Site.

Other than as listed above, your data is not shared with, sold to, or rented to third parties, except where required by law.

6. Retention Period

Your personal data is retained for as long as necessary for the purpose it was processed, subject to any statutory retention/limitation periods under applicable law.

7. Your Rights (KVKK Art. 11)

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to: learn whether your personal data is being processed; request information about such processing; learn the purpose of processing and whether your data is used accordingly; know the third parties, in Turkey or abroad, to whom your data is transferred; request correction if your data is incomplete or inaccurate; request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions set out in KVKK; request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom your data was transferred; and object to a result that is to your detriment arising solely from automated analysis of your data.

To exercise these rights, you may send your request by email to dytserragor@gmail.com.

8. Cookies

The Site uses cookies placed by Google Ads to measure advertising performance. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in applicable law or in our site practices. The current version is always published on this page.
